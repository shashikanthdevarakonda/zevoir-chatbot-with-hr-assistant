ZEVOIR TECHNOLOGIES - HR GUIDELINES

1. Working Hours
Standard working hours are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday through Friday, with a 1-hour lunch break.

2. Attendance
Employees are expected to log their attendance daily through the HR portal.
Repeated unexplained absence may result in disciplinary action as per company policy.

3. Communication Etiquette
Employees are expected to inform their reporting manager in advance for any planned leave, work-from-home request, or late arrival.
For unplanned situations such as illness, employees should inform their manager as early as possible on the same day, via message, call, or email.

4. Late Arrival
Employees arriving more than 30 minutes after standard start time should inform their manager and mark the reason in the attendance portal.

5. Grievance Redressal
Employees can raise workplace concerns confidentially with HR via the internal HR helpdesk.

6. Performance Reviews
Performance reviews are conducted twice a year, in line with the appraisal cycle communicated by HR.
