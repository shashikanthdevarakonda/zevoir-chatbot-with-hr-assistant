ZEVOIR TECHNOLOGIES - COMPANY LEAVE POLICY

1. Casual Leave (CL)
Employees are entitled to 12 casual leaves per year, credited at 1 leave per month.
Casual leave must be applied for at least 1 day in advance through the HR portal, except in genuine emergencies.
A maximum of 3 consecutive casual leave days can be taken without special approval from the department head.

2. Sick Leave (SL)
Employees are entitled to 8 sick leaves per year.
A medical certificate is required if sick leave is taken for more than 2 consecutive days.
Sick leave may be applied for on the same day of absence by informing the reporting manager.

3. Earned / Privilege Leave (EL)
Employees are entitled to 15 earned leaves per year, accrued monthly.
Earned leave can be carried forward up to a maximum of 30 days.
Earned leave requests must be submitted at least 7 days in advance for approval.

4. Leave Application Process
All leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal and approved by the reporting manager before the leave is considered confirmed.
Unapproved absence will be marked as Leave Without Pay (LWP).

5. Public Holidays
Public holidays are governed separately under the Holiday Calendar policy and are not deducted from casual, sick, or earned leave balances.
