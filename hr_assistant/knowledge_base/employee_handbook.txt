ZEVOIR TECHNOLOGIES - EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK (OVERVIEW)

1. About Zevoir Technologies
Zevoir Technologies is a technology company delivering AI, automation, and software solutions to clients globally.

2. Code of Conduct
Employees are expected to maintain professionalism, honesty, and respect toward colleagues, clients, and partners at all times.
Confidential company and client information must not be shared outside the organization.

3. Reporting Structure
Every employee reports to a designated reporting manager, who is the first point of contact for leave approvals, WFH requests, and day-to-day queries.
Escalations beyond the reporting manager should be directed to the HR team.

4. Probation Period
New employees undergo a probation period of 3 months from their date of joining, during which performance and conduct are reviewed.

5. Resignation and Notice Period
Employees are required to serve a notice period of 30 days upon resignation, unless otherwise agreed in writing with HR.

6. IT and Asset Usage
Company laptops, accounts, and software must be used strictly for official work purposes and returned upon exit.

7. Anti-Harassment Policy
Zevoir Technologies maintains a zero-tolerance policy toward workplace harassment of any kind. Complaints can be raised confidentially with HR.
