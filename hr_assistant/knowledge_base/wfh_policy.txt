ZEVOIR TECHNOLOGIES - WORK FROM HOME (WFH) POLICY

1. Eligibility
Employees may work from home with prior approval from their reporting manager.
WFH requests should be informed at least 1 day in advance, except in cases of illness or emergency, where same-day intimation is acceptable.

2. WFH Limits
Employees can avail a maximum of 8 work-from-home days per calendar month.
Any requirement beyond 8 days per month must be approved by the department head on a case-by-case basis.

3. Working from a Different Location
Employees who wish to work remotely from a location outside their base city (for travel, personal reasons, etc.) may do so for up to 15 consecutive days with prior HR and manager approval.
Requests to work remotely for longer than 15 consecutive days require special written approval from HR and are evaluated individually; there is no automatic entitlement beyond this period.

4. Availability
Employees working from home are expected to be reachable and available online during standard working hours, 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM IST, unless otherwise agreed with their manager.

5. Equipment and Connectivity
Employees are responsible for ensuring a stable internet connection and a suitable working environment while working from home.
